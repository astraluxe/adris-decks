Before you bid - check these three

  1. Who can bid: some tenders accept only the maker or an OEM-authorised seller.
  2. Experience and turnover asked, and whether MSE / startup exemptions apply.
  3. Delivery: installation at site, the days allowed, and how long the buyer takes to pay.
