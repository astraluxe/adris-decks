The risk

Where patient test reports leak

What we usually see - tell us where we are wrong.

01

Files travel unlocked

Patient test reports often move by email, chat and USB as plain files that anyone who receives a copy can open.

02

Shared folders stay open

Shared drives and folders are usually opened for one task and rarely closed again, so too many people can read too much.

03

A lost laptop is a leak

A laptop or phone that goes missing usually carries copies of files that were never meant to leave the office.
